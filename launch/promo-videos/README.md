# Launch promo videos

Thirty-second promos for the four OKJTech products, and a launch reel that introduces all four. They're made for phones (1080×1920, 9:16), loop cleanly, and each one uses its own product's colours, fonts, logo and original music.

| Video | For | Look and sound |
|---|---|---|
| `videos/naoa.mp4` | Couples and wedding planners | Ivory and cinnamon, Cormorant Garamond and Great Vibes; warm afro-pop |
| `videos/najenga.mp4` | Homeowners, managers, contractors | Warm off-white, near-black and wine-crimson, Figtree, site-plan grid; steady build |
| `videos/tfe.mp4` | Football fans and travel partners | Pitch-black and TFE red, Figtree, pitch markings; stadium beat |
| `videos/tenafi.mp4` | Hosts, hotels and local businesses | Ink and TenaFi yellow, Figtree, signal waves; afro-house groove |
| `videos/okjtech-reel.mp4` | The launch overview | "Four products. One team." then ~6.7 s of each, ending on all four logos |

Every promo has the same build: the product's logo and tagline, ten features at 2.5 s each (one bar of music, so every cut lands on a downbeat), then the logo again with a call to action. Devices stay still and only the screens move: an iPhone for phone screens, a laptop for dashboards.

## Where the screens come from

All screens are real ones from the products. None are mock-ups.

- **Naoa:** Naoa's marketing screenshots and clips (`frontend/public/marketing` in the Naoa repo).
- **Najenga:** its dashboard screenshots (`public/marketing`), a phone snapshot from its visual tests, and its live public pages.
- **TFE:** its fan, partner and school screens (`public/assets/img/landing-v2`) and its live public pages.
- **TenaFi:** its guest and host phone screens (`public/legacy/assets/v2`, plus the two report screens on tena-fi.com/hosts) and its live public pages.

Before posting, check that the claims still hold, such as TenaFi's pricing and TFE's AFCON 2027 packages. TenaFi's video has no host dashboard screen. If one becomes available, add it to `products/tenafi/screens` and use it in `product.js`.

## Changing and re-rendering

`player.html` is the shared Three.js player; each product's `product.js` holds its theme, scenes and screen script. Open the player in a browser (served over HTTP from a product folder after one `render.sh` run) to watch it live, with sound, chapters and keyboard arrows.

Requirements: Node with Playwright and its Chromium, Python 3 with numpy and scipy, and ffmpeg.

```bash
cd launch/promo-videos/tools
```

```bash
./render.sh tenafi preview "3.6,11.2,26.4"
```

```bash
./render.sh tenafi
```

```bash
./reel.sh
```

- **preview:** renders stills at the times you give into `tools/prev`.
- **full render:** writes `videos/<name>.mp4`. It takes about 15 minutes with software WebGL.
- **reel:** rebuilds `videos/okjtech-reel.mp4` from the four videos.

Fonts are downloaded from Google Fonts on the first run. The music is generated by `tools/music2.py` (Naoa's by `tools/music_naoa.py`); both are seeded, so they give the same track every time.
